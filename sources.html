SOURCES & METHOD

Intelligence should have context.

ECONOVA is built around multiple information layers rather than one signal.

Market Data

Live and delayed market feeds are used to establish current instrument context.

Economic Events

Macro calendars, releases and market-moving events are part of the event layer.

Institutional Positioning

Positioning data such as COT can be used as longer-horizon context.

Market Structure

Structure and liquidity concepts provide the technical context layer.

AI Intelligence

AI is used to synthesize context and explain signals, not to guarantee market direction.

Transparency

Sources and methodology will be expanded as each intelligence module moves into production.
